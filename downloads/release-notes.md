## Deliberate limits

- No live scoreboard, future-result update, arbitrary parameter slider, odds-market calibration or official-team endorsement.
- 2026 inputs are retrospectively reconstructed and not strictly verified point-in-time snapshots.
- τ and the committee model have not been independently calibrated; scenario envelopes are not confidence intervals.
- No licensing-sensitive historical match table, unpublished internal dataset, personal Drive link or full 100,000 season trajectory archive is shipped.
